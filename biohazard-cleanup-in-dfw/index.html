Contact CSI when the scene involves more than an ordinary spill.

Avoid touching, moving, or attempting to clean materials when you are unsure of the source, extent, or potential exposure.

Why household cleaning may not be enough

The goal is not to make the scene look clean. It is to address it correctly.

Effective remediation depends on identifying affected materials, controlling movement through the space, addressing the source, and preventing contamination from being spread elsewhere.

01

Hidden Migration

Fluids can travel beneath flooring, behind trim, into seams, and through gaps that are not immediately visible.

02

Cross-Contamination

Shoes, tools, hands, clothing, and improper movement can transfer contamination beyond the original area.

03

Material Limitations

Some porous or heavily affected materials may not be appropriate for surface treatment alone.

04

Source-Level Odor

Odors generally return when the affected source material has not been identified and appropriately addressed.

Frequently asked questions

What clients often ask about biohazard cleanup.

What is considered a biohazard cleanup?

Biohazard cleanup may involve blood, bodily fluids, decomposition, contaminated contents, trauma scenes, hoarding-related sanitation concerns, or other biological contamination requiring specialized assessment and remediation.

Should I clean the area before CSI arrives?

No. Avoid touching, moving, or attempting to clean affected materials. Limit access to the area and prevent pets, children, employees, tenants, and visitors from entering.

Can you clean carpet, furniture, and mattresses?

These materials can be assessed, but whether they can be appropriately remediated depends on their construction, porosity, contamination level, and the extent of migration.

Do you remove contaminated materials?

When materials cannot be appropriately remediated, removal may be included in the recommended scope. The required work depends on the scene and affected materials.

Can CSI address persistent odors?

Yes. Odor treatment begins with locating and addressing the source. Masking products alone generally do not resolve odors caused by biological contamination.

Do you work with landlords and businesses?

Yes. CSI works with property owners, landlords, property managers, hotels, businesses, rental operators, fleet managers, and other authorized contacts.

Do you provide biohazard cleanup throughout DFW?

CSI serves the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex and surrounding North Texas communities. Contact us to confirm availability for your city and situation.

Will my inquiry remain confidential?

CSI treats service inquiries and scene details with discretion. Share only the information needed for us to understand the situation and determine the appropriate next step.
