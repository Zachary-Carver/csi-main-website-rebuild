Fort Worth cleanup questions

Frequently asked questions about biohazard cleanup in Fort Worth.

Does CSI provide crime scene cleanup in Fort Worth?

Yes. CSI evaluates crime scene, trauma, blood, unattended death, decomposition, hoarding, odor, and vehicle cleanup requests throughout Fort Worth and nearby Tarrant County communities.

When can cleanup begin after a crime or death?

Cleanup may generally begin after law enforcement or the appropriate authority releases the scene and an authorized property contact grants access.

Do you serve Fort Worth apartments and hotels?

Yes. CSI works with apartments, rental homes, condominiums, hotels, extended-stay properties, businesses, and managed locations.

Can CSI coordinate with building management or security?

Yes. CSI can coordinate authorized access, keys, entry codes, loading areas, elevators, escorts, parking, and management contacts.

Can CSI clean vehicles in Fort Worth?

Yes. CSI provides biohazard cleanup for personal vehicles, rental cars, rideshare vehicles, company vehicles, commercial vehicles, and fleets.

Does insurance cover biohazard cleanup?

Some policies may provide benefits depending on the incident, policy language, exclusions, deductible, authorization, documentation, and insurer decision. Coverage is not guaranteed.

Can property managers request vendor documents?

Yes. Contact CSI to request available company, insurance, transporter, safety, or vendor-review information.
