Everybody has bloodstains.

They do not have to be literal. Grief. Trauma. Illness. Addiction. Betrayal. Burnout. Failure. Losing someone. Losing yourself. The bloodstain is what happened. The breakthrough is what you choose, learn, build, change, or become afterward.

Your story did not end with the bloodstain.

The space after survival

You survived it. Nobody taught you what came next.

Bloodstains & Breakthroughs exists for that part. Not to compare pain. Not to force positivity. Not to make you relive everything. To ask what survival built, what no longer serves you, and what you want to build now.

What are your bloodstains?

What marked you is not the same thing as what defines you.

Everybody carries something. Bloodstains can be visible or invisible, recent or decades old, dramatic or deeply private. There is no ranking system here. Pain does not need to win a contest before it counts.

The point is not to make the bloodstain your identity. The point is to recognize what happened, understand what it shaped, and decide what gets to happen next.

The question at the center of everythingWhat happened to you... and what did you do next?

Surviving was step one

Survival mode can save you. It can also become the only language you know.

People teach us how to get through the emergency. Hold on. Push through. Keep moving. Be strong.

Then the emergency ends, the room gets quiet, and almost nobody tells you how to live when you are no longer just trying to make it through the day.

Bloodstains & Breakthroughs occupies that gap: survival → understanding → rebuilding → forward movement.

01 · UNDERSTAND

What did survival create?

Patterns, strengths, fears, instincts, identities, and habits that once had a job to do.

02 · REBUILD

What still fits?

Keep what protects your values. Question what only protected an old emergency.

03 · MOVE

What comes next?

Not a perfect ending. A deliberate next step you can actually live.

A breakthrough can be quiet

  • choosing to stay

Why Christina created this

Homicide victim's sister. Crime Scene Investigator. CEO. Still here.

Former Crime Scene InvestigatorFounder & CEOPodcast hostSpeakerAuthorAdvocate

Christina Hester spent years examining what happened at literal crime scenes. Her own life also required her to rebuild through loss, illness, career change, and major life disruption.

Bloodstains & Breakthroughs asks the question that kept following her beyond the scene: What happens to the people who are still standing afterward?

This platform was created from Christina's story, but it is not only about Christina. It exists because everybody has their own bloodstains, and surviving something does not automatically teach you how to live beyond it.

The community-side bridge

St;ll Here Society

Bloodstains & Breakthroughs is about the story and the rebuilding. St;ll Here Society is about not rebuilding alone.

Bloodstains & Breakthroughs asks, “What happened, and what happens next?” St;ll Here Society answers, “You are still here. Now what do we build from here?” It is a community extension centered on connection, belonging, and continued rebuilding. It is not a paid membership and it is not therapy.
