Bloodstains & Breakthroughs

Everybody has bloodstains.

Maybe yours are grief, trauma, illness, addiction, betrayal, burnout, failure, losing someone, or losing yourself. The bloodstain is what happened. The breakthrough is what you decide comes next.

Your story did not end with the bloodstain.

What we are building

From surviving it to building from it.

Bloodstains & Breakthroughs is becoming a home for practical workshops, trainings, live conversations, reflection tools, and honest conversations about what happens after survival mode.

No trauma Olympics

This is not about who had it worse.

Everybody carries something. This space is about recognizing what marked you, understanding what survival built inside you, and deciding how you want to move forward.

It is not about erasing the past or pretending it did not matter. It is about refusing to let the hardest chapter become the entire book.
