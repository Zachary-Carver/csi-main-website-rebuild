Avoid touching, cleaning, moving belongings, entering restricted areas, or transporting contaminated items before receiving guidance. Wait for law enforcement release when applicable.

Contact questions

Frequently asked questions before the first call.

Do I need to clean before contacting CSI?

No. Do not attempt to clean or prepare the affected area. The current conditions help CSI understand the scope and risks.

Can I send photographs?

Contact CSI first. Photographs may sometimes help with an initial review, but they do not replace an assessment and should never be taken by entering an unsafe or restricted area.

When can cleanup begin after a crime or death?

Cleanup may begin after the appropriate authority releases the scene and authorized property access is available.

Do you work with landlords and businesses?

Yes. CSI works with homeowners, families, landlords, property managers, businesses, hotels, rental operators, employers, fleet managers, estate contacts, and other authorized representatives.

Can you provide an estimate over the phone?

Some initial information may be discussed by phone, but an accurate scope depends on the scene, affected materials, access, contamination, property conditions, and required work.

Do you accept insurance-related projects?

CSI can communicate with authorized property and claim contacts. Coverage and reimbursement depend on the individual policy, deductible, carrier, authorization, and claim decision.

Will my inquiry remain private?

CSI treats inquiries with discretion and communicates only with appropriate authorized contacts involved in the service.
